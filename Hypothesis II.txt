Hypothesis:

- When test data is noisy, CNN models experience a more rapid performance decline; this is because CNNs operate via multiple layers—meaning that noise affecting the initial layer propagates and amplifies through subsequent layers, causing the model's performance to deteriorate quickly. In contrast, traditional machine learning models rely on a single layer, so errors do not spiral out of control.

- As sigma increases from 0 to 0.3:
  
  + The drop in accuracy due to image noise for a CNN model can range from 10% to 40%.

  + The extent of accuracy degradation due to image noise for traditional ML models ranges from 5% to 25%.
